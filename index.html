the name came first. sharp — like crystal, like a compiler warning, like the edge of a clean build. the wolf came later, from the way a pack works: nobody gets left on the floor, and somebody always carries the spare batteries.

i spend most of my life in a terminal — distro-hopping, running linux on a phone that was never supposed to run it, shipping small tools nobody asked for. sharp is the other half of that: the part that goes outside, gets photographed, and lets strangers believe a wolf writes code.

design notes: crystal crest down the spine, white-to-grey gradient on the fur tips, ears that read as "always listening" and a tail that reads as "always in the way".

"same person, better ears."
